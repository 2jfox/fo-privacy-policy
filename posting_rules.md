# Forum Online Posting Rules

**Last Updated:** March 10, 2026

These rules explain what users may and may not post on Forum Online.

By submitting or publishing content on Forum Online, users agree to follow these Posting Rules, the [Safety Policy](./safety_policy.md), and the [Content Moderation Policy](./content_moderation_policy.md).

## What Forum Online Is For

Forum Online is for lawful discussion, proposals, debate, and participation around public-interest and governance topics. We allow disagreement, criticism, satire, and controversial viewpoints, but not abuse, threats, fraud, or exploitation.

## You May Post

- opinions, arguments, and criticism
- proposals and comments for public discussion
- lawful advocacy and campaigning
- links to relevant articles, research, or evidence
- satire and parody, as long as it is not deceptive impersonation

## You May Not Post

- child sexual abuse material, grooming, or sexualized content involving minors
- threats, violent incitement, or celebration of serious harm
- instructions for serious illegal acts
- hate speech or degrading attacks on protected groups
- harassment, bullying, stalking, or coordinated abuse
- pornographic sexual content or sexual services promotion
- scams, phishing, impersonation, fake fundraising, or fraudulent schemes
- malware, harmful links, or attempts to steal access or data
- passport scans, identity numbers, addresses, phone numbers, or other private personal data
- copyrighted content you do not have the right to share
- repeated spam, flooding, or manipulation of the discussion

## Extra Rules For Proposals And Political Content

- Be honest about what your proposal does.
- Do not impersonate public institutions, election bodies, journalists, or other users.
- Do not use the platform to suppress, threaten, or intimidate other participants.
- Do not encourage violence, sabotage, or illegal coercion.
- Do not ask other users to publish sensitive personal documents.

## Moderation And Consequences

Forum Online may:

- hold submissions for review
- reject or remove content
- disable comments or reactions
- suspend or ban accounts

Serious violations may be escalated to relevant authorities.

## How To Report A Problem

You can report problematic content through:

- the in-app reporting flow on posts
- safety@forum.online

## Final Note

Identity verification does not give anyone special permission to break the rules. The same standards apply to all users.

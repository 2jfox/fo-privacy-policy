# Forum Online Safety Policy

**App Name:** Forum Online  
**Developer:** Forum Online  
**Last Updated:** March 10, 2026

Forum Online contains user-generated content, including posts, proposals, comments, profile text, and links shared by users. We are committed to maintaining a safe environment while allowing lawful civic discussion and debate.

## Zero-Tolerance Categories

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate removal, account suspension, permanent banning, and reporting to relevant authorities where appropriate:

- Child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexualization of minors, or any child exploitation content
- Credible threats of violence, violent incitement, or terrorism-related promotion
- Content that facilitates serious illegal activity
- Non-consensual sexual content or sexual extortion
- Doxxing, exposure of private personal data, or targeted harassment

## Other Prohibited Content

Forum Online does not allow:

- Hate speech or degrading attacks against protected groups
- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, or stalking
- Spam, scams, phishing, impersonation, or coordinated manipulation
- Pornographic sexual content or sexual services promotion
- Graphic gore posted to shock or abuse other users
- Malware, harmful links, or attempts to compromise accounts or devices
- Copyright or trademark infringement

## Reporting And Review

Users can report content through:

- The in-app reporting flow available on posts
- Email: safety@forum.online

We review safety reports on an ongoing basis. High-risk safety reports, including child safety, credible threats, and severe abuse, are prioritized for rapid review and we aim to take action as quickly as possible, typically within 24 hours.

## Enforcement Actions

Depending on severity, context, and repeat behavior, Forum Online may:

- Reject content before publication
- Remove published content
- Disable comments or interactions on a post
- Restrict account capabilities
- Suspend or permanently ban accounts
- Block abusive users from continued use of the service
- Escalate to law enforcement or child-protection organizations when required

Verified identity or passport-based access does not exempt any user from moderation or enforcement.

## Community Standards

Forum Online is built for civic and governance discussion. Controversial opinions, criticism, and political disagreement are not removed simply for being unpopular. Moderation focuses on safety, legality, abuse prevention, privacy protection, and platform integrity.

# Forum Online Content Moderation Policy

🌐 Read this in other languages: [Русский](content_moderation_policy/content_moderation_policy.ru.md) | [Українська](content_moderation_policy/content_moderation_policy.uk.md)

**Last Updated:** March 10, 2026

This policy explains how Forum Online moderates user-generated content. It is designed to support a civic discussion product that also meets mainstream app store expectations for safety, reporting, and ongoing moderation.

By submitting or publishing content on Forum Online, users agree that their content may be reviewed and enforced under this policy, the [Safety Policy](./safety_policy.md), and the [Posting Rules](./posting_rules.md).

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all user-generated content on Forum Online, including:

- posts
- proposals
- comments and threaded replies
- profile names, bios, and other profile text
- links, attachments, and other submitted metadata

## 2. Moderation Principles

Forum Online aims to protect lawful expression while enforcing clear safety boundaries.

We do not moderate simply because content is controversial, politically unpopular, critical of institutions, or emotionally charged. We do moderate when content creates safety risk, violates law, abuses other users, invades privacy, or undermines the integrity of the service.

Our moderation is guided by these principles:

- safety first for children and severe abuse
- legality and platform integrity
- privacy and anti-doxxing protection
- anti-spam and anti-scam enforcement
- proportional enforcement when context matters

## 3. Content Subject To Action

Forum Online may reject, remove, restrict, or escalate content involving:

- child sexual abuse material, grooming, or child exploitation
- credible threats, violent incitement, terrorism advocacy, or praise of mass harm
- instructions or facilitation for serious illegal activity
- targeted harassment, stalking, intimidation, or dogpiling
- hate speech or degrading attacks against protected groups
- doxxing, publication of passport scans, identity numbers, addresses, phone numbers, or other private personal data
- non-consensual sexual content, sexual extortion, or exploitative sexual content
- spam, scams, phishing, impersonation, coordinated deception, or fraudulent fundraising
- malicious links, malware distribution, or attempts to compromise accounts or devices
- repeated low-quality flooding designed to manipulate discussions or rankings
- copyright or trademark infringement

## 4. Special Standards For A Voting And Governance App

Because Forum Online is used for proposals, voting, and civic discussion:

- strong political opinions are allowed if they remain lawful and non-abusive
- advocacy must not include threats, coercion, or targeted harassment
- proposals must not promote violence, criminal conduct, or fraud
- users may not post forged credentials, fake endorsements, or misleading content that impersonates a person, institution, or authority
- users may not publish personal identity documents, passport images, or other sensitive identifying material

## 5. Pre-Publication And Post-Publication Moderation

Forum Online may use a combination of pre-publication review and post-publication enforcement.

This can include:

- submissions held for moderation before becoming public
- reactive review after user reports
- manual review by moderators
- account-level review for repeat abuse or evasion

## 6. Reporting

Users can report content through:

- the in-app report flow on posts
- safety@forum.online

Reports should include as much detail as possible. We review reports on an ongoing basis and prioritize urgent safety issues.

## 7. Enforcement Actions

Depending on severity and context, Forum Online may:

- warn the user
- reject a pending submission
- remove content
- freeze comments or interactions on a post
- limit visibility or features
- suspend an account
- permanently ban an account
- preserve evidence and refer the matter to relevant authorities

Severe child safety, credible threat, or violent abuse cases may skip warnings and go directly to removal and permanent enforcement.

## 8. Repeat Offenders And Evasion

Users who repeatedly violate rules or attempt to evade moderation may face stronger penalties, including long suspensions, permanent bans, and blocking from continued use of the service.

## 9. Appeals

If a user believes content was removed or restricted in error, they may contact:

- safety@forum.online

We may request additional context and may uphold, reverse, or modify the original decision.

## 10. Policy Maintenance

This policy may evolve as the product grows, legal requirements change, and new abuse patterns appear. Material changes may be reflected in updated in-app references or website policy pages.
